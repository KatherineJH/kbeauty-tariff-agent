15. Cotton or man-made fiber apparel goods of Nicaragua.
(a) The rate of duty provided for in subheading 9915.61.01 in the "Special" subcolumn of rates of duty column 1 shall apply
to goods of Nicaragua, in an aggregate quantity not to exceed the annual total quantity set forth in subdivision (c) of this
note. Subheading 9915.61.01 applies to the cotton or man-made fiber apparel goods described in this note if the goods
meet the applicable conditions for preferential tariff treatment under general note 29 to the tariff schedule, other than the
condition that they be originating goods and are both cut or knit to shape, and sewn or otherwise assembled, in the territory
of Nicaragua.
