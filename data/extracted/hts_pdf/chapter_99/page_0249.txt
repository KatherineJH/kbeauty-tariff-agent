subchapter, the term “modules” shall include the following goods provided for in subheading 8541.43.00 of the tariff
schedule: a module is a joined group of CSPV cells, as such cells are defined in subdivision (c) of this note, regardless
of the number of cells or the shape of the joined group, that are capable of generating electricity. Also included as a

interconnection (including, but not limited to, assembly into a laminate). Such CSPV cells assembled into modules or made
