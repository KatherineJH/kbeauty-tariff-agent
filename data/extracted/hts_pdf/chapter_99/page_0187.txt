Liechtenstein subject to a specific or compound rate of duty under column 1-General, the ad valorem equivalent rate
of duty of such good shall be determined by dividing the amount of duty payable under column 1-General by the
customs value of the good. For example, if a good were subject to a specific duty of 50 cents per kilogram, and one
kilogram of the good were entered with a customs value of $10, then the ad valorem equivalent rate of duty would be
obtained by dividing 50 cents by $10, yielding 5 percent. For any good of South Korea for which a specific or compound
rate of duty under column 1-Special is properly claimed, the ad valorem equivalent rate of duty shall be determined
in the same manner.
