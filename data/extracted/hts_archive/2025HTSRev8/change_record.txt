Change Record
The modifications set forth in this change record reflect updates made to the HTS after 2025 Revision 7,
published April 5, 2025. Please note that the Change Record does not include staged rates, endnotes or
minor nonsubstantive adjustments to the format of any line. The modifications set forth in this change
record reflect updates made to the HTS since 2025 Revision 7 was posted:
Chapter 99.
Notes: For purposes of this record, the terms “added” and “deleted” refer to the insertion or removal of
8‐digit (legal) tariff rate lines or legal notes; the terms “established” or “discontinued” refer to the
insertion or removal of 10‐digit (nonlegal) statistical reporting numbers or notes. The term “annotated”
refers to the creation of new 10‐digit provisions under a previously unsubdivided rate line; the term
“restored” refers to the deletion of all annotations under an existing rate line. The term “modified” may
be used with changes in any provision or note.
